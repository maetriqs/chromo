    Moves

    • Double jump — press jump again within ~1.4 s of leaving the ground.
    • Flip / dodge — second jump while holding a direction. Forward flips hit hardest and give a burst of speed.
    • Wall driving — drive into any curved wall to ride up it. You stick to walls and the ceiling while your wheels are down.
    • Powerslide — hold it while steering for tight turns at speed.
    • Aerials — jump, tilt the nose up (hold S) and boost.
    • Stuck on your roof? Press jump to flip back over.
    • Boost pads — small pads give 12, the big glowing orbs refill to 100.
    • Demolitions — ram an opponent at supersonic speed to blow them up for 3 s.

    Other

    Gamepad

    Browser tip: Ctrl+W closes the tab in Chrome and Edge. While a match runs the game asks before leaving, and in fullscreen the keyboard is locked so the shortcut reaches the game. You can also rebind powerslide.
