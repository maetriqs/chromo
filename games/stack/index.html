How to play

A block slides back and forth above the tower. Drop it with a click, a tap or Space. Anything hanging over the edge is sliced off, so the tower gets narrower.

Land a block almost exactly on the one below for a perfect drop. Three perfect drops in a row start growing the block back. Miss the tower completely and it's over.
