How to play

Each number tells you how many of the eight squares around it hide a mine. Uncover every safe square to win. Your first dig never hits a mine.

Right-click, or switch to Flag, to mark a mine. Click an uncovered number whose mines are all flagged to open its other neighbours at once. With the keyboard, press F to flag the focused square.
