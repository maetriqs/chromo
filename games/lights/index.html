How to play

Pressing a lamp flips it and the lamps directly above, below, left and right of it. Lit ones go dark and dark ones light up.

Turn every lamp off. Par is the number of presses used to set the puzzle, so matching it is good and beating it is better. Each new puzzle takes a few more presses.
