How to play

Tip the board to roll the red ball to the green goal. Use ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D, or press on the board and drag the way you want the ball to go. Let go to level it out.

Each level is a bigger maze. From level 3, dark holes appear; roll into one and the ball goes back to the start.
